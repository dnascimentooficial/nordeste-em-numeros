Why I did this study

I did this study out of anger, and also out of pride. Anger at a discourse that keeps repeating that the Northeast is the same as ever, erases the region's history and ignores what states and municipalities did and failed to do. Pride in a region that has changed more than people say, even if a long road remains. So I checked the official data, from 2000 to today, and the history, from the colonial era to now. Nobody gets a free pass here: municipalities, states and the federal government share the bill. Every number has its source and calculation in plain view, for anyone who wants to rebut it or understand it.
